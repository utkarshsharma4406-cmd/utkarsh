Dental receptionist demo

A small local demo. No API key, paid AI service, or model download is needed.

Please use fictional details. Replies come from simple local rules, not a generative AI model. The app keeps contact details in the Codespace's memory until it stops. Optional microphone transcription is handled by your browser and may be sent to the browser vendor's speech service; use typing if you do not want to use that feature.

    The Speak button uses browser speech recognition when available. Replies are spoken with a voice available to your browser. Speech features and microphone permissions vary by browser and device.
